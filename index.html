01 / ABOUT

Ideas are easy. Putting the pieces together is the fun part.

The Echelon Young Innovators Hackathon 2026 is an inter-school hackathon for students of Classes 9 to 12, hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad.

Students form small teams, pick a problem worth solving, and build a working prototype. Teams that clear the online round come to campus for a nine-hour finale and present what they have built to industry mentors and judges.

You do not need to have done this before. Bring a team, an idea and some curiosity. We will help you work out the rest.

02 / TRACKS

Five tracks. Find where your idea fits.

Choose a track when you register. If your idea does not slot neatly into one of the first four, Open Innovation is where it belongs.

  • AI & Smart Tech

    Machine learning, automation and smart devices put to work on a real problem.

  • Education & Digital Learning

    Tools that make learning easier, cheaper or available to more students.

  • Sustainability & Environment

    Ideas that cut waste, save energy or help people live more lightly.

  • Health, Safety & Society

    Projects that keep people healthier, safer or better connected to help.

  • Open Innovation

    The piece that fits nowhere else. If the idea is good, bring it.

06 / FAQ

Questions

Tap a question to open it. Still stuck? The numbers to call are at the end.

Who can participate?

Students of Classes 9 to 12 only, in teams of two to four. All members of a team must be from the same school.

When and where is the finale?

21 November 2026, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, at Echelon Institute of Technology, Faridabad.

Is the whole event in person?

No. The first round runs online from 31 October to 5 November 2026. Only shortlisted teams attend the finale on campus.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Taking part is completely free.

Can I take part on my own?

No. The minimum team size is two.

Do I need to know how to code already?

Some coding ability in the team helps, but the hackathon is built for Class 9 to 12 students and beginners are very welcome. Mentors are there to help on the day.

I have a question that is not answered here. Who do I ask?

Call the Student Coordinator, Yogita, on +91 78276 69804, or the Faculty Coordinator, Mr. Pranav Bhati, on +91 70654 37032.

What are the themes or problem statements?

There are five tracks: AI and Smart Tech, Education and Digital Learning, Sustainability and Environment, Health, Safety and Society, and Open Innovation. You pick one when you register.

How do we register?

Use any Register button on this page. It opens the official listing on Unstop, where one member signs up and adds the rest of the team. Registration is open from 12 October to 30 October 2026.
